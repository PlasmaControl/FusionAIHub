Detachment: lower outer divertor leg

Review the lower outer leg using the producer’s EFIT shelf gate. Without DRSEP, the shelf gate does not establish magnetic topology. TangTV corpus images are 50 Hz linear resamples: blends of adjacent exposures, previewed at ≤20 fps.

Attached
the strike point carries the full heat and particle flux
Detached
the radiating front has left the plate (partial or full)
MARFE
the front has moved above the X-point onto the confined plasma
Uncertain
indicators disagree, or all valid ones sit in a transition band
Absent (unassessed)
fewer than two indicators were valid: nothing can be said

These are the producer’s state definitions verbatim. MARFE is also physically detached, but has its own exclusive stage. Missing data is never attachment. Afrac = 1/DOD: high Afrac (≥0.75) supports attached, falling Afrac (≤0.5) supports detached; 0.5–0.75 abstains. Afrac is uncalibrated and self-referenced to this shot’s 0.90 quantile, so an always-detached shot can appear attached in its top tail; treat it as a weak indicator. Prad,div ≤0.35 supports attached and ≥0.50 detached; it measures radiation, not detachment. TangTV DZ <0.35 supports attached, 0.5–1.0 detached and >1.0 MARFE, only inside the producer’s shelf gate. A surrogate DZ is a regression estimate. A valid abstention is grey; invalid bins are hatched with their reason at the shared time. The primary label model and fallback rule are unverified suggestions.
