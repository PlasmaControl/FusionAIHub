Drag across the figure to select a time range, then mark it. The scroll wheel and the modebar zoom the whole figure; every pan and zoom re-renders the visible window at full resolution on the server, and double-click returns to the whole shot.

What Save writes

One new file, review/<shot>__<reviewer>__<stamp>.csv, in the same five-column schema as format/. Corrections are append-only: every press writes its own file and nothing under review/ is ever overwritten or deleted, so a second reviewer cannot destroy the first's work.

It writes nothing else. Not shots.csv — that is edited by hand, and tier and holdout in it are curation calls nothing here derives. Not format/ — merging rows into it is a manual step. And no raw data is promoted to EKOLEMEN; that is labeler-raw promote, on purpose.
