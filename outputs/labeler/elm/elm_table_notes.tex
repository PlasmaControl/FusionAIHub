\paragraph{ELM evaluation notes.}\label{app:elm-table-notes}

\textbf{Sets.} \emph{all119}: the 119 reviewed shots, 12,409 interior 50 ms bins (wholly inside one reviewed absent, non-crowd or crowd span and inside signal coverage), so boundary-straddling bins are dropped and the task is easier than whole-shot detection. \emph{bes73}: the 73 of them with BES, 6,843 bins (elm-elmo's coverage). \emph{Common bins} (11,653/6,527) also need valid DSM rows. \emph{Known-majority cells}, used only in the legacy swap, are 50 ms cells with at least 25 ms of legacy coverage that at least 25 ms of one reviewed state, present or absent, labels; mixed, uncertain and not-observable cells stay out and unknown time stays unknown. \emph{Exact exports} are the source DSM's saved native rows. $^{\ddagger}$ marks upstream data reused in source training, normalization or selection (two blind-cohort shots); $^{\dagger}$ marks recall $\geq0.99$.

\textbf{Names.} \texttt{elm-elmo} is our reimplementation of ELM-O; \texttt{elm-dsm-survival} the DSM survival refit on source 1 ms rows; \texttt{elm-dsm-detect} the 60-input $1\times128$ occupancy-detection adaptation on 50 ms means; \texttt{elm-dsm-native-detect} the 124-input [100,1000] detection refit on 1 ms means.

\textbf{Labels.} 97\% of positive bins lie in crowd spans (ELMing periods, not isolated ELMs); non-crowd starts are annotation boundaries without independent physical-onset truth. The review began from the clock: 56\% of crowd starts, 44\% of ends and 33\% of both lie within 1 ms of its boundaries (86 crowd spans), which favours D-alpha-input models over elm-elmo. Of 93 non-crowd starts on 22 BES shots, 63 (on 15 shots) match the nearest elm-elmo onset within $\pm50$ ms; reviewed start minus elm-elmo onset has median $-20$ ms and quartiles $[-28, -12]$ ms, an annotation offset, not a physical-onset error.

\textbf{Thresholds and alarms.} Each fold selects its checkpoint by inner-validation AUPRC and its threshold by inner-validation F1; elm-ours thresholds a bin's mean probability, elm-elmo and the clock use any touching detected span, and the DSM adaptation thresholds one aligned row score. FPR is the fraction of absent bins called present; the alarm is the fraction of covered absent spans touched by any detection, and guard25 trims 25 ms from each absent-span edge first. The centered 50 ms mean carries elm-ours detections about 25 ms across reviewed edges and the clock's edges seeded the review, so the raw alarm favours the clock; guard25 narrows but does not remove that advantage. Alarm raw/guard25: elm-ours 0.596/0.427, clock 0.105/0.096 on all119; elm-elmo 0.440/0.307 on bes73. Fold 3 (zero-based) of elm-ours selected epoch 2 of 25 (fold thresholds 0.170--0.761); four training seeds give all119 AUROC 0.924--0.949. On non-crowd-only shots (13 shots/1,003 bins) elm-ours precision is 0.298 [0.132, 0.549]. Intervals use 1,000 shot-bootstrap draws and need five denominator-bearing shots per endpoint.

\textbf{Moved-boundary block.} It keeps bes73 spans whose boundaries both moved more than 1 ms from the clock (43 of 84 present and 117 absent spans; 4,125 bins on 57 shots); with so few present spans its intervals are wide and the comparison inconclusive.

\textbf{Panels.} Native-detection panel: 37 shots with complete 124-input rows (48 pass the 112/124-input gate). The native refit trains on 24--28 shots per fold with 2--8 inner-validation shots, against 81--82 and 14 for the headline elm-ours; the elm-ours row on the native folds' shots separates training size from architecture. Legacy swap: eight overlap shots, seven with BES; the legacy table and the DSM were built on WPQH phases with breakthrough-ELM targets. Finding 1 is read on 782 known-majority cells ($|M|=60$, $|P|=14$; legacy recall 0.692, precision 0.906); the 641 strict interior bins of the method rows give $|M|=40$, $|P|=4$. The swap shots' PCPHD02/03 come from the upstream export; a fresh fetch reproduces it sample for sample (16 of 16 records), so the input source does not explain the DSM detection gap on those shots. Smith windows are selected windows: precision and F1 there are conditional, and continuous-discharge precision is unavailable.

\textbf{Limits.} Cross-validation is developmental: predictions on outer-fold shots existed before the recipe was fixed, and 16 of 94 run days cross folds. Folds grouped by run day, which hold every day whole but do not balance annotation kinds, give all119 AUROC 0.944 [0.916, 0.964] and F1 0.860 [0.811, 0.900] against 0.941 and 0.830 for the shot-grouped headline, which stays the headline. Serving runs each shot whole through the five fold models, with the mean fold threshold (0.417, unevaluated); 4,096 ms tiles with frozen weights give AUROC 0.932 against 0.941 (whole minus tiled +0.009 [-0.002, +0.024]). The fast-density input (native ordinate over $10^{14}$) has median 0.84, 5--95\% range 0.17--1.93 and 0.4\% of cells at the upper clip (12) over 1--4 s, so the clip alters few cells. Fast-density ordinate units and FS01--04 sightlines are unverified (no sightline list is retained in the signal records or the literature digests); the U-Net's input scaling and chord screen are numerical choices, not calibrations. The DSM detection rows are lower bounds on DSM detection skill under our recipe. A frozen-model blind-split score awaits review of those shots; no catalog onset is delivered.
